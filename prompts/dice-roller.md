# Dice Roller

Build me a dice roller for tabletop nights.

I want to pick a die type — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, or d20 — choose how
many of them to roll at once, and hit a big satisfying ROLL button.
Show the individual results as big chunky numbers, and the total in
enormous type. Keep a short history of previous rolls so I can settle
arguments later. Bonus points for a little shake animation on the dice
when they land.

Rules: everything in one self-contained HTML file, no libraries, and
it should feel fun to use.
